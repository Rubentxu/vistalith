# Product Requirements Document

## Primary jobs
1. Ask about a software system conversationally.
2. Navigate the same subject through code/architecture/decisions/evidence.
3. Sketch hypotheses and architecture alternatives.
4. Delegate analysis to multiple agents.
5. Compare alternatives and traces.
6. Promote an idea into governed SDDK work.
7. Observe execution and verification live.
8. Explain why the system reached its current state.
9. Resume without reconstructing everything from chat.
10. Control models, providers, MCP, permissions and budgets transparently.

## MVP differentiator
Do not compete by implementing the deepest editor first.

Prove:
- semantic chat;
- graph;
- C4;
- visual intent;
- SDDK governed transition;
- agent/workflow trace.
