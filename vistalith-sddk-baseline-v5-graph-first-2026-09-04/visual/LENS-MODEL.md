# Lens Model

A lens is a projection and interaction vocabulary over shared semantic subjects.

Initial lenses:
- Chat;
- Thinking;
- Architecture/C4;
- Workflow;
- Agents;
- Decision;
- Evidence;
- Impact;
- Traceability;
- Timeline;
- Trace/Usage;
- UAT;
- Code;
- Terminal.

## Cross-lens operations
- select;
- focus;
- reveal;
- ask why;
- explore;
- compare;
- promote;
- preview;
- act.

## Lens invariant
A lens cannot authoritatively mutate SDDK state.
