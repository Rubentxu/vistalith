# C4 / Architecture Lens

## Renderer
LikeC4 remains the preferred first-class C4 renderer/model adapter.

## Capabilities
- system context;
- containers;
- components;
- dynamic views;
- sequence views;
- drill-down;
- cross-lens selection;
- architecture diff;
- provenance;
- impact overlays;
- decision/evidence overlays.

## Graph mapping

Architecture subjects are SWG nodes; C4 is one projection.

Do not force requirements, tests, agents or decisions into LikeC4 as their
canonical storage model.
