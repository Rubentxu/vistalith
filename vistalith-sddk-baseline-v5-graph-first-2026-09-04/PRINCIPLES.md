# Principles

1. **SDDK is the engineering kernel.**
2. **The graph is the shared semantic workspace.**
3. **The trace is the explanation, not the chat transcript.**
4. **Chat is the primary door, not the authority.**
5. **Subjects are more important than files or renderer node IDs.**
6. **Relationships are first-class data.**
7. **Proposals are not effects.**
8. **Derived knowledge carries provenance and confidence.**
9. **Unknown impact fails visibly rather than pretending certainty.**
10. **Agents contribute structured knowledge, not only prose.**
11. **Visual thinking supports progressive formalization.**
12. **Time, alternatives and rejected paths remain inspectable.**
13. **A renderer never defines domain semantics.**
14. **Rig and MCP are implementation mechanisms, not the product model.**
15. **A Vistalith innovation enters SDDK only if it serves SDDK's own focus.**
16. **No second workflow, decision, evidence or authority kernel is created in Vistalith.**
17. **Local-first before cloud-first.**
18. **Architecture emerges from measurable pain and UAT evidence.**
